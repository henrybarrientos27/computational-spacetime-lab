spacetime and extra-dimensional mechanisms to test, ran the calculations, and
is responsible for understanding and accurately representing the results.

OpenAI Codex made substantial contributions to implementation, derivations,
tests, documentation, literature routing, and claim boundaries. The models are
based on published physics and public data; they are not original laws merely
because they were assembled in this repository.

The defensible contribution is an auditable computational lab that translates
ambitious ideas into explicit assumptions, numerical bounds, failure gates, and
reproducible negative results. Henry should not claim discovery of a new
dimension, portal, warp drive, wormhole, or law of physics.
