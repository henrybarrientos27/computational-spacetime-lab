# Theory Summary

Barrientos Shortcut Law is currently framed as a testable nonlinear-resonance modeling idea.

The original motivation involved shortcut-like transport, but the current research version is narrower: test whether a nonlinear system under certain resonance/load conditions enters a threshold-dependent state where a correction term improves the baseline model.

## Current Focus

- Nonlinear resonance
- Threshold behavior
- Loaded system states
- Frequency and amplitude sweeps
- Correction-term modeling
- Delay/loss/transfer comparison

## Important Limitation

This project does not claim to prove 4D travel, faster-than-light travel, or a replacement for modern physics. The current goal is to make the idea measurable and testable.
